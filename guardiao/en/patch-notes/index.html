PATCH NOTES

What changed in each version of Forest Guardian.

  1. Version 1.0.24

    October 25, 2026 Latest
    • Saci Day, until Nov 7: catch the whirlwind in the Clearing and get the Saci's Cap for free.
    • Catalysts: 6 new evolutions, made with a passive instead of a 2nd weapon.
    • New garden: swipe to harvest and tend; pole beans give three harvests.
    • The Hornero pair builds two upgrades at once.
    • Every place in the Clearing awakens with its own scene and music.
    • Leaderboards by Danger, showing each run's guardian.
    • The daily gift no longer resets if you skip a day.

  2. Version 1.0.22

    October 23, 2026
    • New Caatinga Clearing: win on Nightmare Danger in the Caatinga and bring the backlands to your camp.
    • Run music that builds with the fight, and forest sounds in the Clearing that follow the time of day.
    • The game opens faster and adjusts graphics quality on its own when your phone struggles (Options › Graphics quality).
    • On your way out of the Clearing, the trail reminds you to bring a dish from the kitchen.
    • Fix: buttons no longer play two sounds at once.

  3. Version 1.0.21

    October 23, 2026
    • Fix: the game no longer freezes when the Inferno evolution burns the ground.
    • Remixed sound: the boss music comes in on the beat, and a heartbeat warns you when health is low.
    • Heavier hits, clearer elemental effects, and the boss now rises from a pool of the Rot.
    • The forest has mushrooms that glow at night, and the game runs lighter.
    • Share your run card and invite friends to play.
    • Full-screen display adjusted for Android 15.

  4. Version 1.0.19

    October 20, 2026
    • New title screen and a redesigned end-of-run screen showing each weapon's share of damage.
    • Leaderboards on Google Play Games with a top-3 podium: score and highest Danger, weekly and all-time.
    • Cloud progress: sign in to Play Games and your progress comes back when you reinstall or switch phones.
    • News notifications. If Android blocks them, Options shows it and one tap fixes it.
    • Ancestral Clearing with redone animations and a new garden.

  5. Version 1.0.18

    October 20, 2026
    • New title screen: the clearing at night, with the moon, hills and forest, and a crackling campfire.
    • Leaderboards on Google Play Games: score and highest Danger, weekly and all-time.
    • Cloud progress: sign in to Play Games and your progress comes back when you reinstall or switch phones.
    • News notifications. If Android blocks them, Options shows it and one tap fixes it.
    • Ancestral Clearing with redone animations and a new garden.

  6. Version 1.0.17

    October 20, 2026
    • New title screen: the clearing at night, with the moon, hills and forest, and a crackling campfire.
    • Leaderboards on Google Play Games: score and highest Danger, weekly and all-time.
    • Cloud progress: sign in to Play Games and your progress comes back when you reinstall or switch phones.
    • News notifications (you can turn them off in Options).
    • Ancestral Clearing with redone animations and a new garden.

  7. Version 1.0.16

    October 20, 2026
    • Leaderboards on Google Play Games: score and highest Danger, weekly and all-time.
    • Cloud progress: sign in to Play Games and your progress comes back when you reinstall or switch phones.
    • News notifications (you can turn them off in Options).
    • Ancestral Clearing with redone animations and a new garden.

  8. Version 1.0.15

    October 20, 2026
    • Leaderboard: if your Play Games profile is private, the game tells you that only you can see your score and how to appear for everyone.
    • Small text fix on the leaderboard.

  9. Version 1.0.14

    October 20, 2026
    • Fix: scores now reach the Play Games leaderboard. Your best run that was waiting is sent automatically when you open the game.

  10. Version 1.0.13

    October 20, 2026
    • Fix: the leaderboard now always shows the up-to-date list, including your latest run.
    • If a run can't reach Play Games, the leaderboard tells you and lets you try again.

  11. Version 1.0.12

    October 20, 2026
    • Play Games: if it doesn't sign you in automatically, the game asks once when it opens. Signing in from anywhere turns on cloud progress and leaderboards right away.
    • Fix: a run played before signing in to Play Games now shows up on the leaderboard as soon as you sign in.

  12. Version 1.0.11

    October 19, 2026
    • Google Play Games leaderboards: Score and Highest Danger cleared, weekly and all time. Open them from the trophy in the Clearing or by tapping your points at the end of a run (solo runs only).
    • Cloud progress: sign in to Play Games and your progress comes back when you reinstall or switch phones.
    • Fix: with large text, the victory line no longer overlaps the title.

  13. Version 1.0.10

    October 19, 2026
    • News notifications (optional): turn on Options › Game › News to hear about new versions and events, a few times a month. Chest and harvest reminders stay separate.
    • Notifications now use the game's sprout icon.

  14. Version 1.0.9

    October 19, 2026
    • Ancestral Clearing garden (Founder Pack): a fence of carved stakes woven with straw, a gate with copper finials, a sprout sign and lanterns swaying in the wind, and a new scarecrow in a woven poncho, with a blue feather in its hat and a lantern on its arm.

  15. Version 1.0.8

    October 19, 2026
    • Ancestral Clearing (Founder Pack) with redone animations: blue morpho butterflies with real flight and shadows, landing on the flowerbeds; a livelier toucan; a banner in the wind; lanterns swaying in the breeze; mushrooms that breathe at night.

  16. Version 1.0.7

    October 19, 2026
    • New: Wardrobe in the Clearing (freeing, trail and Awakening effects; free pieces and kits) and a Shop with the limited-time Founder Pack.
    • Founder's Ancestral Clearing: carved posts, lanterns that light up at night, a toucan, blue morpho butterflies and glowing mushrooms.
    • Joystick: move right after picking a card, anywhere on your half of the screen.

  17. Version 1.0.6

    October 19, 2026
    • New: Wardrobe in the Clearing — freeing, trail and Awakening effects, with free pieces and kits (Pink Ipê, Silk Floss Tree, Burrowing Owl, Festa Junina). Looks only.
    • New: Shop with the limited-time Founder Pack: No Ads, all 4 kits and the exclusive Ancestral Clearing.
    • Joystick: now starts anywhere on your half of the screen, not just at the bottom.

  18. Version 1.0.5

    October 19, 2026
    • Fix: the joystick stopped working after picking a card, pausing or reviving.
    • "No Ads" purchase: the game now tells you when your payment is approved and when a purchase is refunded.
    • Clearing: "No Ads" shop button at the top, and the corner panel now fits large text.

  19. Version 1.0.4

    October 16, 2026
    • New: optional "No Ads" purchase — ad rewards are yours instantly, no watching.
    • Accessibility in Options: softer flashes, fixed, left-handed and larger joystick, assisted fishing, and danger warnings easier to see for colorblind players.
    • Zoom now works in the Clearing too, and fishing is a little easier.
    • Fix: the Options button no longer turns into a line when tapped.

  20. Version 1.0.3

    October 15, 2026
    • New: Zoom in Options (accessibility) — the world looks bigger on screen while the interface keeps its size.
    • Fishing: taps now work anywhere on the screen.

These are the same notes as on Google Play, newest first.
