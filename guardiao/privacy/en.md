# Privacy Policy — Forest Guardian (Guardião da Floresta)

*Last updated: October 9, 2026*

**Forest Guardian** ("the game", `com.lopesleo.guardiao` on Google Play) is developed by
Leonardo Lopes, publishing as **Komedy Games Studio**. This policy explains what data the game
uses, why, and what choices you have. This is a translation; the Portuguese version is the
reference: <https://lopesleo.github.io/guardiao/privacidade/>.

## Summary

- The game is **free** and has **optional ads** (you choose to watch one to earn a
  reward). There are never ads during the action.
- We don't ask for an account, name, email, phone number or location.
- Your progress stays **on your device**.
- We use Google services (AdMob, Firebase and Google Play) that process some technical
  data, described below.

## Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, unlocks, achievements, records) and settings (volume, vibration,
language) are saved **only in your device's local storage**. They are not sent to our
servers and are deleted when you uninstall the game or use the "Erase progress" option in
the Options menu.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows **optional rewarded ads** through **Google AdMob**. To show and measure
ads, AdMob may use your device's **advertising ID**, IP address, device information
(model, system, language) and data about how the ad was shown. Google processes this
under its own policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

- You can **reset or turn off the advertising ID** in your Android settings
  (Google › Ads).
- For users in the **European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland**, the
  game shows a **consent message** (Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time it
  opens. You choose whether to accept personalized ads and can change your choice later in
  the game's privacy options.
- The **"Remove ads"** purchase (when available) removes full-screen ads and grants the
  rewards without watching.

## Usage and error analytics (Firebase)

We use **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** to understand how the game is
played (for example, run length, level reached, which rewards are viewed) and to fix
crashes. The data is **technical and anonymous to us** (installation identifier, device
model, app version, game events and error reports). We don't link this data to any
person. You can turn it off at any time in
**Options › Privacy › Usage and error data**.

## Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is processed by **Google Play**. We **do not** receive or store
payment data; we only receive confirmation that the purchase was made.

## Network connections

- **Ads and analytics:** described above.
- **Time check:** when online, the app makes a simple request to a Google address
  (`clients3.google.com/generate_204`, the same one Android uses to test the connection)
  only to read the correct date and time and prevent cheating in real-time activities
  (garden, building, daily gifts). **No player data is sent.**

## Permissions

- **Internet:** for ads, analytics and the time check.
- **Advertising ID:** used by AdMob (see above); can be turned off in Android.
- **Vibration:** haptic feedback on taps and events (can be turned off in Options).
- **Notifications (optional):** only if you accept, to let you know the daily chest or
  the harvest is ready. Can be turned off in Options.

## Children

The game is intended for **people over 13** and is not directed at children. We do not
knowingly collect data from children.

## Your rights (LGPD and GDPR)

You can request information about the data processed, its correction or deletion, and
withdraw consent at any time. Since we don't keep accounts or personal data on our own
servers, most of it is already under your control on the device (erase progress, reset the
advertising ID, turn off notifications).

## Changes to this policy

If anything changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
